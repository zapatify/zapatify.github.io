## Featured Projects

Here are some of the projects I've been independently working on since leaving IOU Financial.  Browse the navigation to explore them in more detail.  

- **Strategic Advantage Network (SAN) Platform** (Private Repo) - Technical Consultant: Architected and designed AI-powered strategy platform for Clarifi Solutionz enabling SMBs to transform business strategy into executable roadmaps. Designed comprehensive technology architecture including data ingestion pipelines, AI-assisted insight engine, strategy memory system, and execution orchestration capabilities. Platform leverages anonymized cross-company data for benchmarking and continuous learning, creating defensible market intelligence for member organizations. Demonstrated ability to translate complex business requirements into scalable technical architectures supporting strategic consulting workflows.
- **JobZappy** - AI-powered resume optimization SaaS platform, that I created out of neccesity.  As someone with a breadth of experience, I wanted to focus on leadership roles.  Whether they be data engineering, or software development, I wanted to create an application which would accept all my experience, and tailor that that experience to the leadership role.  I have since handed this over to my son to bring this to market.  
- **ZappyNotes** - Comprehensive note-taking application with subscription tiers that I created once I saw how much my Evernote subscription went up.  I assumed there were others who experienced the same sticker shock and developed a much cheaper alternative that I later released as open-source in favor of other projects.  
- **TKTrakker** - Discord bot for gaming analytics and team tracking.  I developed this way back in my gamer days, to assist our teams in tracking team kills in Discord.  This go around I used Claude to aid me in pushing it to a hosting service, and add it to my Discord server.  This was more of a POC for me.  
- **Amortizy** - Ruby gem for loan amortization calculations.  One of the challenges we faced at IOU Financial is that the amortization engine was not flexible, and implementing the features the business was asking for required a pretty hefty lift (which never reached a top priority).  I developed a feature-rich amortization engine from the ground up, and released it as a Ruby gem, that could fill that gap for any SMB lender today.  
- **Fear, Faith, and the Forest** - During this unemployment journey, I've found a lot of bad energy, negativity and hopelessness.  I decided to put some of my positive energy into a Podcast which guides professionals through unemployment with positivity, hope and faith.  

Each project demonstrates different aspects of modern software development, from SaaS architecture and payment processing to open-source libraries and real-time systems. The podcast represents my commitment to helping others navigate career transitions with strategy and faith.
